Cashwise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Overview

Cashwise is a personal finance dashboard for iOS. The app is designed to help you track accounts, transactions, budgets, subscriptions, and savings goals without advertising tracking or selling personal data.

Data You Add

Cashwise stores the financial information you choose to enter, including account names, balances, transactions, budget categories, subscription details, savings goals, preferences, and receipt-derived transaction details when you use receipt scanning.

Storage and Sync

Cashwise stores app data on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled and available, your data may sync through your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. Apple handles iCloud data according to Apple’s own privacy terms.

Photos, Camera, Biometrics, and Notifications

Cashwise asks for camera or photo access only when you choose to scan or import a receipt image. The app may ask for notification permission to send budget, subscription, or savings reminders. If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is handled by iOS; Cashwise does not receive or store your biometric data.

Tracking and Advertising

Cashwise does not use third-party advertising tracking, does not sell your data, and does not use the IDFA advertising identifier.

Data Sharing

Cashwise does not share your app data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data may be processed by Apple services such as iCloud when you use Apple platform features.

Your Choices

You can delete app data by removing records inside the app or deleting the app from your device. You can manage iCloud, camera, photo, notification, and biometric permissions in iOS Settings.
